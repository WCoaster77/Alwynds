What's working well

  • Clear audience split. Separate Owners and Tenants sections make it easy for each visitor to find their path.
  • Transparent pricing. Publishing fees on "How We Help Owners" builds trust. Many competitors hide their pricing.
  • Credibility signals. BCFSA and PAMA logos, a team page with photos and bios, and a physical Broadway office address.
  • Useful tenant content. The showing-preparation tips and FAQ answers are practical.
  • Consistent listing format. Every listing shows the same key facts: price, beds, baths, pets, parking, size and availability.

01

Rent increase figures are out of date and don't match each other

  • The Owners FAQ quotes the 2020 maximum (2.6%).
  • The Tenants FAQ quotes the 2023 maximum (2.0%).
  • Both should show the current year's figure, or link to the RTB page instead of stating a number.
02

Rented listings are hard to tell apart from available ones

About 46 listings are online. Only 3 have an availability date after the review date: 930 Cambie, 5477 Wales Street and 1631 Dunbar Street, all November 1, 2026. The rest show past dates, some from 2020–2022.

  • The listing editor in WordPress has a "Rented" toggle. It places a small white "RENTED" label in the bottom-left corner of the listing photo.
  • That label is an image file (rented.png) with no alt text. The word "Rented" doesn't appear in the page's text, so search engines and screen readers can't tell the home is rented.
  • The label is small and sits in a corner of a busy photo, so visitors can easily miss it.
  • The sign also appears on the listing cards. But the cards' text never says "Rented": the Available row still shows a date (e.g. August 1, 2022 on 1618 Quebec Street), which can read as "available from" even when the photo says rented. Rented homes stay in the main listings alongside available ones.
  • Fix: add alt text to the image in the theme template: <img class="rented" src="…/rented.png" alt="Rented">
  • Update the Available field: when the Rented toggle is on, show "Rented" in the Available row instead of the date, on both the cards and the listing page. The field may currently accept only a date, so this would be a small change in the theme template (if the toggle is on, show "Rented"; otherwise show the date) rather than something staff enter by hand.
  • Make it easier to see: enlarge rented.png (a bigger version of the image, or a larger display size in the theme's CSS) so visitors notice it.
  • Also worth doing: have the same toggle add "(Rented)" to the page title and meta description, move rented homes out of the main listings or sort them last, and remove them from the XML sitemap.

Suggestions for the new site

  • Build on the existing Rented toggle: keep the rented.png badge with alt text, and use the toggle to hide or archive rented listings automatically.
  • Add listing filters for city, price, bedrooms, pets and type.
  • Use one rental application, ideally an online form, and link it everywhere.
  • Explain PCA and the tenant guarantee on the Owners page.
  • Replace hard-coded legal figures with links to official sources, or set a yearly reminder to update them.
  • Keep the transparent pricing table. It's a strength.
  • Plan a simple article schedule, even a few posts a year, to keep the site current.
